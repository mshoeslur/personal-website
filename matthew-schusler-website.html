04 — Philly

A Local's Guide to Philadelphia

Not a listicle — this is my actual rotation. Five neighborhoods, the city-wide favorites, and where to stay if you're visiting.

$ under $20/person $$ $20–50 $$$ $50–100 $$$$ over $100
  1. 1

    Queen Village

    Accessible · near Bella Vista, Old City, and the Delaware

    Standouts: Royal Tavern, Grace and Proper, Scampi, Herman's Shot Tower.

  2. 2

    Fitler Square

    Close to 30th Street Station, the Schuylkill River Trail, Rittenhouse, and the museums

    Standouts: Illata, Pine St. Grill, Trattoria Carina, Mr. Rabbit, Rowhome.

  3. 3

    Rittenhouse

    Expensive, city-central, great food, multiple transit options

    Standouts: Parc, Dandelion, Dizengoff, ABar, Elixr.

  4. 4

    Passyunk

    Old-school South Philly · great food · on the Broad Street Line

    Standouts: Second District Brewing (Comfort Food Sundays), Bomb Bomb, Bok Bar, Artisan Boulanger, Fountain Porter.

  5. 5

    Fishtown

    Unique, hipster, great food, on the Market-Frankford Line — a bit disconnected from the rest of the city

    Standouts: Kalaya, Suraya, Laser Wolf, Gilda, Clubhouse.
